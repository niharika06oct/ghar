Ghar · Palette Reference

Warm Modern

A UI palette for Ghar's app chrome (buttons, cards, text, tags), formalized from the colors already living in ghar-core.js and ghar.css. It sits alongside the five watercolor render palettes, and is built to harmonize with them, not replace them.

Strategy: earthy Indian-warm core (terracotta clay + teak) anchored on tinted-warm neutrals, with one cool secondary (coastal teal) for balance and gold/marigold as reserved highlights.

Two flags: White text on Terracotta (4.4:1) and Clay Taupe on cream (4.45:1) both land just under the 4.5:1 body-text threshold. They pass for large text (≥18.66px bold / 24px). For small body text, use Terracotta only at heading sizes (or swap to Teak / Burnt Sienna, both 6–8:1), and darken Clay Taupe to #6e655a when it carries small copy. Gold and Marigold take dark text only — white on them fails.
